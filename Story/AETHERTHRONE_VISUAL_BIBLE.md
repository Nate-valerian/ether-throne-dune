# AetherThrone — Visual and Character Bible

## Visual identity

`AetherThrone` is an ancient cosmic epic, not a conventional clean science-fiction world. Images should feel monumental, ceremonial, old, quiet, and physically real.

- Aspect ratio: 2.39:1 cinematic widescreen
- Frame rate target: 24 fps
- Camera language: slow movement, symmetrical imperial framing, wide environmental scale, restrained close-ups
- Lighting: naturalistic environments contrasted with cold Aethari illumination
- Materials: black stone-metal, pale ceramic, smoked glass, aged gold, suspended dust, fabric with architectural weight
- Avoid: familiar Earth military uniforms, modern screens, neon cyberpunk cities, rubber forehead aliens, exaggerated fantasy armour, direct visual copies of another franchise

## Are the Aethari human?

No. The Aethari are an ancient non-human species with a humanoid body plan. At a distance, they can resemble very tall humans. At close range, their proportions, eyes, skin, movement, and voices make the difference unmistakable.

Humans later resemble them partly because both species share an upright, tool-using form and partly because Serin uses selected Aethari developmental patterns when accelerating Earth's native hominids. Humans are not Aethari descendants or hybrids; they remain an Earth species whose development was altered.

## Aethari anatomy

### Height and build

- Typical adult height: 2.15–2.40 metres / 7'1"–7'10"
- Exceptional military height: up to 2.55 metres / 8'4"
- Build: long-limbed and narrow-waisted, with dense musculature rather than fragility
- Arms: slightly longer relative to the torso than human arms
- Hands: five long fingers with dark translucent nails
- Neck: long and upright
- Posture: extremely controlled; Aethari elites rarely fidget or make unnecessary movements

### Face

- Face is recognizably expressive but subtly elongated
- High, smooth forehead and pronounced cheek structure
- Nose is narrow with a shallow bridge
- Ears lie close to the skull and taper slightly backward
- Teeth are small and uniform, with no exaggerated fangs
- Facial expressions tend to be restrained, making small emotional changes significant

### Eyes

- Slightly larger than human eyes
- Sclera appears black or extremely dark graphite
- Iris is a thin metallic ring: silver, pale gold, blue-white, or copper
- Pupil expands until it nearly consumes the iris during Fold exposure
- Eyes reflect light like polished volcanic glass
- Do not give them glowing eyes continuously; glow appears only during technology use or Fold contact

### Skin

Aethari skin has a mineral-like iridescence visible only when light moves across it. Natural tones include:

- pearl-grey;
- ash bronze;
- pale stone;
- graphite;
- deep umber with silver undertones.

Fine luminous markings appear at the temples, throat, and inner wrists. They are biological sensory structures, not tattoos. Their brightness increases near Fold technology.

### Hair

- Hair colours: black, silver, white, charcoal, muted bronze
- Texture ranges from straight to tightly curled
- House elites often wear long hair gathered or structured ceremonially
- Soldiers commonly shave the head or keep hair very short
- Navigators traditionally have shaved heads so neurological and Void-Dust damage remains visible

### Voice and movement

- Their voices contain a subtle harmonic undertone, as though a second quieter note accompanies speech
- They move slowly when in control and with sudden precision when threatened
- Ceremonial stillness is a marker of status
- Visible hurried movement suggests fear, loss of discipline, or social humiliation

## Aethari sex and gender

Aethari have two common reproductive sexes, but physical dimorphism is less pronounced than in humans. Height, authority, clothing, and occupation are not strongly gendered. Their civilization recognizes multiple social genders, though the films do not stop to explain the system.

For audience clarity, the principal cast uses the following identities:

- The Navigator: nonbinary and intentionally androgynous; they/them
- Ambassador Vael: male; he/him
- Doctor Serin Oth: male; he/him
- Archon Maelor Vaethyr: male; he/him
- Lyra Voss: female; she/her
- Commander Kael: male; he/him
- Ena: human female; she/her
- Nia: human female; she/her

Romance is not determined by Aethari gender categories. The Navigator's intimate relationships, if developed, grow from trust rather than conventional social roles.

## Lifespan

- Typical Aethari lifespan: 180–230 Earth years
- Physical maturity: approximately 25 Earth years
- Great House elites can live longer through medical intervention
- Navigators often die much earlier because Void-Dust damages memory and neurological continuity

This lifespan allows the same Aethari characters to witness humanity's creation, abandonment, and rediscovery decades later.

## Principal appearance anchors

### The Navigator

- Species: Aethari
- Gender: nonbinary
- Height: 2.12 metres / 6'11"
- Apparent age: early adult by Aethari standards
- Build: slender, physically weakened by repeated Fold exposure
- Skin: pale stone with a faint blue-silver iridescence
- Eyes: black sclera with very thin silver irises
- Hair: completely shaved
- Markings: branching silver-black Void-Dust damage along the neck, spine, palms, and temples
- Face: symmetrical, observant, emotionally contained; neither strongly masculine nor feminine
- Clothing: layered matte-black robes with a high open collar, no jewellery, no House crest by personal choice
- Movement: cautious after Fold travel; otherwise unusually still
- Key silhouette: a slight dark figure surrounded by enormous circular machinery

### Ambassador Vael

## Human appearance

The first engineered humans are recognizably modern human beings. They must not look like smaller Aethari. Their skin, hair, eyes, body shapes, and facial features are naturally diverse because Serin draws from broad native genetic variation.

- Typical adult height: 1.55–1.90 metres / 5'1"–6'3"
- Human eyes have visible white sclera and non-metallic irises
- Human skin has no iridescence or luminous markings
- Human movement is more expressive, restless, tactile, and emotionally readable than Aethari movement
- Humans frequently touch one another for reassurance, which Aethari observers initially interpret as inefficient behaviour

### Ena

- Gender: female
- Height: 1.70 metres / 5'7"
- Build: strong from physical labour
- Skin: deep brown
- Eyes: dark brown with clearly visible white sclera
- Hair: dense black curls, initially cut short by the laboratory and later allowed to grow
- Face: broad expressive eyes, strong jaw, small vertical scar below the right ear from an obedience implant
- Clothing: undyed practical work layers in clay and charcoal tones
- Key quality: she watches before speaking and develops a warmer physical presence as her independence grows

### Nia

- Gender: female
- Appearance as a child: warm brown skin, dark curls, observant brown eyes, small and quick
- Distinguishing object: half of Ena's metal identification tag worn on a cord
- Appearance as an elder: silver threaded through her curls, weathered face, layered hand-made clothing, same identification tag

## Native hominids in Section 1

These are not yet engineered modern humans. They should resemble plausible archaic hominids rather than apes or fantasy cavemen.

- Height: approximately 1.50–1.70 metres
- Strong, compact bodies adapted to outdoor survival
- Pronounced brow structure and wider faces than later humans
- Dark to medium-brown skin adapted to the region
- Sparse practical coverings made from hide and plant fibre
- Stone tools, naturally maintained fire, cooperative group behaviour
- No spoken modern language, jewellery, architecture, agriculture, or metal
- Their emotional behaviour must be recognizable enough that the audience already sees personhood the empire will ignore

## Scale rule

Whenever Aethari and humans later share a frame, preserve their size difference. An average human's eye line falls around an Aethari adult's upper chest or shoulder. Do not vary this between shots. Doorways, furniture, tools, and ship interiors used by Aethari must reinforce their height.
