# Chapter 1 — Before Humanity

**Film:** I — Children of Earth
**Sections:** 1–10
**Chapter runtime:** 15:00
**Story era:** Earth, before modern humanity

## Continuity anchors

- Earth is prehistoric, immense, fertile, and completely free of artificial structures.
- Aethari are tall non-human beings with subtly elongated features, dark reflective eyes, pale iridescent skin, and fine luminous markings near the temples.
- The Navigator is androgynous, physically slight, with a shaved head, silver-black eyes, and branching marks left by Void-Dust along the neck and hands.
- Vael wears layered pearl-grey diplomatic robes and carries a small black control device he tries not to display.
- Serin wears practical dark laboratory garments with translucent data surfaces around the wrists.
- Aethari technology is monumental and quiet. It uses black stone-like metal, floating rings, cold white light, and no exposed controls.
- The pace is patient and visual. Silence, scale, and reaction shots carry as much meaning as dialogue.

## Section 1 — A World Without a Name

**Runtime:** 00:00–01:30

### 00:00–00:15

Blackness. No music. A field of unfamiliar stars slowly becomes visible.

A low vibration begins—not mechanical, but almost like a voice held below hearing.

### 00:15–00:30

The curve of Earth enters the frame. Sunlight moves across vast oceans and an atmosphere untouched by smoke or artificial light.

**ARCHIVE VOICE:**
“Before humanity gave names to the stars, the stars had already been divided.”

### 00:30–00:45

The camera descends through clouds into a continent of endless forest. Herds of prehistoric animals cross a floodplain. Great winged creatures scatter from the treetops.

There are no roads.

### 00:45–01:00

Night falls in a slow transition. Lightning illuminates mountains. Far below, a small group of native hominids shelter beneath an overhanging rock, pressing together against the storm.

One protects a sleeping infant with its body.

### 01:00–01:15

The sky clears. The hominids look upward without understanding what they see.

**ARCHIVE VOICE:**
“This world possessed no throne, no empire and no recorded prayer. Its people had not yet learned to ask what waited beyond the night.”

### 01:15–01:30

A star brightens above the horizon. It does not move with the sky.

The animals become silent.

**ARCHIVE VOICE:**
“But beyond their world, someone had already answered.”

Cut to black on the first deep note of the score.

## Section 2 — Shapes Beyond the Moon

**Runtime:** 01:30–03:00

### 01:30–01:45

Earth's moon fills the image. Behind it, space bends inward as if reflected through black water.

A silver line appears where no line should exist.

### 01:45–02:00

The line widens into a Fold aperture. Light does not shine from it; surrounding starlight is drawn toward it.

The pointed bow of an Aethari vessel emerges, several kilometres long and perfectly silent.

### 02:00–02:15

More vessels follow: survey ships, carriers and a circular gate structure divided into slowly rotating rings.

Their scale reduces the moon to scenery.

### 02:15–02:30

Inside the lead vessel, pale figures stand before a transparent floor. Earth turns beneath their feet.

No one expresses wonder. Symbols appear over the planet: water, biomass, metals, atmospheric composition.

### 02:30–02:45

A survey official addresses Vael without looking away from the data.

**SURVEY OFFICIAL:**
“Third planet. Active biosphere. No developed civilization. Void-Dust concentration beneath commercial threshold.”

**VAEL:**
“Then why did the route resist?”

### 02:45–03:00

The official has no answer.

Far beneath the ships, the hominid infant wakes and stares at the false star.

One of the vessel's lights pulses.

The infant's eyes reflect the same silver pattern.

## Section 3 — The Great Expansion

**Runtime:** 03:00–04:30

### 03:00–03:15

A black chamber unfolds around a floating map of the galaxy. Thousands of luminous systems are connected by thin silver routes.

The empire appears less like territory than a nervous system.

### 03:15–03:30

Representatives of the Great Houses appear as tall translucent projections. At their centre stands Archon Maelor Vaethyr.

Sol is a dim point at the edge of the map.

**MAELOR:**
“Survey designation.”

**VAEL:**
“Sol. Third planet viable.”

### 03:30–03:45

Icons representing richer systems flare elsewhere—stars with enormous energy yield, worlds positioned along established routes.

**COMPACT REPRESENTATIVE:**
“The system is distant, route-poor and nearly without Dust. There are better investments.”

### 03:45–04:00

Doctor Serin expands the planet's biological analysis.

**SERIN:**
“Its life adapts unusually quickly. The biosphere has survived repeated collapse without external correction.”

**MAELOR:**
“Life survives everywhere. Can it work?”

### 04:00–04:15

Serin displays the silhouette of a native hominid beside an Aethari worker.

**SERIN:**
“Not yet.”

A pause.

“But importing labour would cost more than improving what is already here.”

### 04:15–04:30

Maelor studies Earth for only a moment.

**MAELOR:**
“Perform the survey. If the species can be made useful, claim it for House Vaethyr.”

The point representing Earth changes from white to imperial blue.

## Section 4 — The Last Navigator

**Runtime:** 04:30–06:00

### 04:30–04:45

Deep inside the ship, rotating black rings surround a solitary chair. The Navigator is secured at the wrists, spine and throat.

Void-Dust moves through glass channels like illuminated smoke.

### 04:45–05:00

The Navigator opens silver-black eyes. For an instant, the chamber becomes the Fold: endless pathways formed from light, memory and possible movement.

Earth is visible as a dark sphere surrounded by faint, branching futures.

### 05:00–05:15

A route technician speaks from behind protective glass.

**TECHNICIAN:**
“Transit complete. Fleet integrity ninety-eight percent.”

The Navigator tries to answer but cannot remember the requested word.

### 05:15–05:30

Vael enters and dismisses the technicians. He releases the restraints one at a time.

**VAEL:**
“Tell me what was taken.”

**NAVIGATOR:**
“There was a room at The Null. A window.”

### 05:30–05:45

The Navigator searches for the rest.

**NAVIGATOR:**
“Someone used to stand beside me.”

**VAEL:**
“Do you remember who?”

The Navigator cannot.

### 05:45–06:00

Vael records the loss on a private display: `AUTOBIOGRAPHICAL DEGRADATION — ACCEPTABLE`.

He hides the words before helping the Navigator stand.

Behind them, Earth turns inside the Fold apparatus like an eye that has not opened.

## Section 5 — Vael's Kindness

**Runtime:** 06:00–07:30

### 06:00–06:15

Vael guides the unsteady Navigator through a quiet recovery chamber. He places a warm cup between their hands and waits for the shaking to stop.

His care is practiced and intimate.

### 06:15–06:30

**VAEL:**
“You crossed farther than the physicians recommended.”

**NAVIGATOR:**
“Maelor ordered it.”

**VAEL:**
“And I permitted it.”

### 06:30–06:45

The Navigator notices the small black control device at Vael's belt.

**NAVIGATOR:**
“Did you expect me to refuse?”

Vael covers the device with a fold of his robe.

**VAEL:**
“I prefer not to discover what fear might make either of us do.”

### 06:45–07:00

He opens the observation wall. Earth fills the chamber with blue and green light.

**VAEL:**
“A living world. Almost untouched.”

**NAVIGATOR:**
“You say that as if touching it were inevitable.”

### 07:00–07:15

Vael smiles with genuine sadness.

**VAEL:**
“The Expansion does not believe in distance. Anything reachable eventually becomes necessary.”

### 07:15–07:30

An alert calls Vael to the descent bay.

Before leaving, he places his hand against the Navigator's cheek. After the door closes, the Navigator sees the cup has been designed without a sharp edge, breakable surface, or removable part.

Even comfort has been made safe from freedom.

## Section 6 — First Descent

**Runtime:** 07:30–09:00

### 07:30–07:45

Three survey craft detach from the flagship. Their dark surfaces reflect Earth without showing seams or engines.

The Navigator, Vael and Serin occupy the forward chamber of the lead craft.

### 07:45–08:00

The ships enter the atmosphere inside shells of cold light. Clouds fold around them. A storm bends away from their path.

The Navigator reacts to the living planet with quiet intensity.

### 08:00–08:15

They pass above an ocean crowded with enormous moving forms. Then over volcanic islands, ice fields and forests stretching beyond the horizon.

**SERIN:**
“No planetary management. No climatic regulation. Yet equilibrium repeatedly returns.”

### 08:15–08:30

**NAVIGATOR:**
“You sound offended.”

**SERIN:**
“Impressed. Systems usually require intelligence to remain useful.”

**NAVIGATOR:**
“Useful to whom?”

### 08:30–08:45

The craft approaches a broad valley where a river divides dense forest. Animals scatter beneath its shadow.

Serin marks the location for the first laboratory.

### 08:45–09:00

The vessel stops several metres above the ground. Grass flattens without wind. A black ramp extends.

The Navigator steps onto Earth first.

For a moment, the Fold becomes completely silent.

## Section 7 — A Living Inventory

**Runtime:** 09:00–10:30

### 09:00–09:15

Survey instruments float between trees, surrounding insects, leaves and soil with geometric light. Each living thing receives a number.

### 09:15–09:30

Serin kneels beside a flowering plant and watches it close around his shadow.

**SERIN:**
“Competition at every scale. Cooperation at every scale. This planet cannot decide which principle governs it.”

### 09:30–09:45

**NAVIGATOR:**
“Perhaps both do.”

Serin considers the answer as if it were data.

### 09:45–10:00

A drone maps mineral deposits beneath the valley. The results are ordinary. Another searches for Void-Dust and returns almost nothing.

Vael receives a message from orbit.

**VAEL:**
“The Compact will withdraw if no strategic yield is found.”

### 10:00–10:15

Serin watches a small animal feign injury to lead predators away from its nest.

**SERIN:**
“Biological value may exceed mineral yield.”

**VAEL:**
“Biology is expensive to transport.”

### 10:15–10:30

A survey drone detects movement beyond the river. Heat silhouettes gather beneath stone shelter.

Serin enlarges the image: native hominids sharing food around a natural fire.

“Perhaps,” he says, “transport will not be necessary.”

## Section 8 — The Hominids

**Runtime:** 10:30–12:00

### 10:30–10:45

From behind a field of optical distortion, the expedition observes the hominids. Adults crack roots with stones while juveniles imitate them.

An elder receives food despite contributing nothing to the gathering.

### 10:45–11:00

Serin's instruments trace neural activity, social bonds and genetic variation.

**SERIN:**
“Manual precision. Cooperative learning. Extended care for offspring. Their development is inefficient, but unusually open.”

### 11:00–11:15

A predator moves through high grass toward a juvenile. One hominid sees it and gives a warning cry, drawing the predator's attention toward itself.

The group drives the animal away together.

### 11:15–11:30

**VAEL:**
“Can they understand instruction?”

**SERIN:**
“Not ours.”

**VAEL:**
“That was not the question.”

### 11:30–11:45

Serin overlays a projected future anatomy onto the nearest hominid: altered hands, vocal tract, cortex and upright posture.

The image resembles a modern human.

### 11:45–12:00

The Navigator watches the elder touch foreheads with the rescued juvenile.

**NAVIGATOR:**
“They already understand something.”

Vael asks what.

The Navigator answers, “That a life can belong to someone other than itself.”

## Section 9 — Serin's Proposal

**Runtime:** 12:00–13:30

### 12:00–12:15

Back aboard the survey vessel, Serin builds a human prototype from layers of projected anatomy.

The image rotates before Maelor and the Conclave.

### 12:15–12:30

**SERIN:**
“We accelerate cortical development, refine the hand, strengthen vocal control and preserve environmental adaptability. Mature workers within six local years.”

**COMPACT REPRESENTATIVE:**
“Cost?”

### 12:30–12:45

Serin displays a figure far below the cost of importing Aethari labour.

**KAEL:**
“Will they obey under threat?”

**SERIN:**
“Obedience can be embedded below conscious decision.”

### 12:45–13:00

The Navigator looks toward the living hominids shown on a secondary display.

**NAVIGATOR:**
“Do they remain what they were?”

**SERIN:**
“No organism remains what it was. We will simply choose the direction.”

### 13:00–13:15

Vael senses the Navigator's unease.

**VAEL:**
“Without intervention, they may require a million years to reach language.”

**NAVIGATOR:**
“You speak as if time asked us to interfere.”

### 13:15–13:30

Maelor ends the debate.

**MAELOR:**
“You are discussing tools as though they were citizens. Begin the design.”

The projected human body is stamped with the seal of House Vaethyr.

---

## Section 10 — The Impossible Dream

**Runtime:** 13:30–15:00

### 13:30–13:45

Night covers the valley. The Aethari camp glows beyond the river while the hominids sleep beneath stone.

The Navigator leaves the guarded perimeter alone.

### 13:45–14:00

They approach the shelter without weapons. A sleeping hominid lies near the edge, one hand resting protectively on an infant.

Silver markings awaken along the Navigator's fingers.

### 14:00–14:15

The Navigator touches the hominid's forehead.

The forest disappears.

A rapid series of images begins: hands shaping stone, pigment pressed against a cave wall, bodies gathered around a burial.

### 14:15–14:30

The visions accelerate beyond anything that exists: fields planted in deliberate rows, ships crossing oceans, cities rising, telescopes turning toward the sky.

The Navigator recoils but cannot break contact.

### 14:30–14:45

A human hand reaches toward a window surrounded by unfamiliar machines. Beyond it, stars burn in perfect darkness.

For one instant, the future human appears to look directly back at the Navigator.

### 14:45–15:00

The vision ends. The hominid wakes but does not cry out.

They stare at one another across an impossible distance of time.

From the Aethari camp, the first laboratory descends through the clouds.

**NAVIGATOR, WHISPERING:**
“What are we about to do to you?”

Cut to black.

**END OF CHAPTER 1**
