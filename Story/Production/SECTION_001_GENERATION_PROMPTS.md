# Section 001 — Generation Prompts

Generate `A World Without a Name` as eight separate clips. Keep narration out of the generated footage and add it during editing.

## Shared visual rules

Apply these rules to every prompt:

> Photorealistic monumental cosmic epic, prehistoric Earth approximately 350,000 years ago, physically realistic environments and anatomy, restrained natural colours, slow deliberate camera movement, subtle atmospheric haze, cinematic lighting, 2.39:1 widescreen, 24 fps, high dynamic range, fine organic film grain. No text, subtitles, logos, watermark, modern humans, cities, roads, farms, metal, spacecraft, visible aliens, dinosaurs, fantasy creatures, cyberpunk colours, exaggerated caveman stereotypes or fast action.

## Shot 1 — The unnamed stars

**Duration:** 8 seconds

> An eight-second cinematic opening beginning in almost absolute black. A dense and unfamiliar natural star field slowly emerges as the camera drifts imperceptibly forward through deep space. No recognizable constellations, planets, nebula clouds, spacecraft or artificial lights. The image should feel ancient, immense and silent. Very slow fade from darkness, restrained white and deep-blue stars, profound cosmic scale, locked and meditative composition.

## Shot 2 — Untouched Earth

**Duration:** 14 seconds

> A fourteen-second photorealistic orbital view of prehistoric Earth emerging from darkness into sunrise. The camera moves slowly along the planet's curved horizon from the night side toward daylight. Vast blue oceans, enormous natural cloud systems and untouched continents become visible. Absolutely no cities, artificial lights, satellites, orbital debris or spacecraft. Earth should appear fertile, beautiful, isolated and completely unaware that it is being observed.

## Shot 3 — A world without borders

**Duration:** 14 seconds

> A fourteen-second continuous high-altitude aerial shot travelling slowly from immense ocean cliffs toward an uninterrupted prehistoric forest and a broad winding river valley. Morning mist lies between distant hills. Waterfalls descend from dark cliffs. No settlements, paths, agriculture or human structures. Emphasize enormous natural scale and a world governed only by weather, water and living growth. Slow stable camera, natural blue-green palette, realistic sunlight through clouds.

## Shot 4 — Life before ownership

**Duration:** 13 seconds

> A thirteen-second extremely wide cinematic shot of a prehistoric river floodplain. A herd of realistic ancient elephants moves slowly through tall wet reeds while wild horses and smaller grazing mammals cross in the distance. Predators silently observe from a far ridge without attacking. The animals should feel natural rather than staged. Slow lateral camera movement, morning haze, reflections in shallow water, immense forest beyond the river. No dinosaurs and no modern human presence.

## Shot 5 — The small people

**Duration:** 14 seconds

> A fourteen-second night scene during heavy rain. Beneath a natural rock overhang beside a river, seven plausible archaic hominids shelter around a weak fire. They are compact and strong, approximately 1.5 to 1.7 metres tall, with dark-brown skin, broad expressive faces, pronounced brow structure, coarse dark hair, simple hide and plant-fibre coverings, and a few stone tools. They must feel emotionally intelligent and recognizably personal, not ape-like caricatures. Begin outside in the cold rain and move slowly toward the warm firelight without entering the shelter.

## Shot 6 — Warmth, memory and one another

**Duration:** 12 seconds

> A twelve-second intimate continuation using exactly the same archaic hominid group, faces, clothing, fire and rock shelter from the previous clip. One younger adult carefully gives the last piece of roasted root to an injured elderly woman. Nearby, a dark-skinned adult female with coarse black hair curls protectively around a sleeping infant as cold rain blows into the shelter. Other members quietly make room around the fire. Natural restrained emotion, realistic eye contact and touch, warm amber firelight against deep blue rain, slow eye-level camera movement, no sentimentality or theatrical acting.

Use Shot 5's final frame as a visual reference for this clip if possible.

## Shot 7 — The silence

**Duration:** 8 seconds

> An eight-second tense continuous shot at the edge of the same prehistoric forest after the storm. Rain abruptly stops. Water still drips from leaves, but every animal becomes unnaturally silent. A grazing animal in the foreground raises its head. In the background, the same archaic hominids emerge from their rock shelter and look toward the sky. Leaves become completely still. The camera remains almost motionless while the characters listen. No visible alien object yet.

## Shot 8 — The false star

**Duration:** 7 seconds

> A seven-second locked cinematic night shot looking through wet black tree branches toward a clear horizon. Natural stars drift almost imperceptibly with the sky, but one brilliant silver-white point remains perfectly fixed above the horizon. The stationary light is small, distant and mysterious, not yet visibly a ship. The same hominid silhouettes stand at the bottom of the frame looking upward. During the final second, the false star becomes slightly brighter. Cut immediately to black.

The narration should end exactly as the final light brightens:

> “Above the horizon, one star refused to move.”
