# Section 001 — A World Without a Name

**Film:** I — Children of Earth
**Chapter:** 1 — Before Humanity
**Runtime:** 90 seconds
**Purpose:** Establish Earth before engineered humanity and reveal the first sign of the Aethari arrival.

## Reveal rule

Do not show an Aethari face, body, ship, symbol, or piece of technology during this section. Their presence is represented only by the unmoving false star in the final shot. The restraint makes their physical reveal in Sections 2 and 3 feel consequential.

## Narration

Read slowly, with restrained emotion and deliberate silence between paragraphs. Target spoken duration is approximately 68–75 seconds, leaving the remaining time for atmosphere and the final silent image.

> Before humanity named the stars, the stars had already been divided.
>
> The world below possessed no throne, no empire and no recorded prayer. Oceans moved without borders. Forests grew without permission. Creatures lived and died beneath a sky that had never carried a command.
>
> Along a river, a small people survived through warmth, memory and one another. They did not know their world would one day be called Earth.
>
> They did not know distant minds had measured its waters, weighed its living flesh and found it almost worthless.
>
> On the final night before the Expansion, the animals fell silent.
>
> Above the horizon, one star refused to move.

## Timed visual sequence

### Shot 1 — Before the first word

**Time:** 00:00–00:08
**Visual:** Absolute black slowly reveals a dense, unfamiliar star field. No titles and no recognizable constellations.
**Camera:** Almost imperceptible forward drift.
**Audio:** Silence for four seconds, then a sub-bass harmonic begins.
**Narration cue:** Begin “Before humanity…” at 00:06.

### Shot 2 — Untouched Earth

**Time:** 00:08–00:22
**Visual:** Earth's sunlit curve emerges from darkness. Rich blue ocean, enormous weather systems, no artificial lights, satellites, or orbital debris.
**Camera:** Slow orbital movement from night toward dawn.
**Audio:** Low harmonic with distant natural wind texture.
**Narration:** First paragraph.

### Shot 3 — A world without borders

**Time:** 00:22–00:36
**Visual:** High aerial passage from ocean cliffs into uninterrupted forest and a broad river valley. Morning mist fills the lowlands.
**Camera:** Slow, high-altitude forward glide.
**Audio:** Wind, distant water, first restrained string note.
**Narration:** “The world below possessed no throne…”

### Shot 4 — Life before ownership

**Time:** 00:36–00:49
**Visual:** Prehistoric mammals cross a floodplain: a herd of straight-tusked elephants with smaller grazing animals moving through reeds. Predators observe from a distant ridge. No dinosaurs.
**Camera:** Very wide lateral movement emphasizing scale rather than animal close-ups.
**Audio:** Water, insects, distant animal calls.
**Narration:** “Oceans moved without borders…” through “…never carried a command.”

### Shot 5 — The storm shelter

**Time:** 00:49–01:03
**Visual:** Rain at night. Beneath a natural rock overhang, a small group of archaic hominids shelters around a weak fire. Their compact bodies, broad faces, hide coverings, and stone tools are visible without making them look primitive in emotion.
**Camera:** Begin outside the shelter, then slowly move closer without crossing into the firelight.
**Audio:** Rain, fire, quiet breathing. Music almost disappears.
**Narration:** “Along a river, a small people survived…”

### Shot 6 — The recognizable choice

**Time:** 01:03–01:15
**Visual:** One adult gives the last piece of food to an elder. Another curls protectively around a sleeping infant as cold rain blows inward.
**Camera:** Intimate close shots at eye level; no sentimental slow motion.
**Audio:** Fire crackle and a soft four-note musical idea, not yet Ena's full melody.
**Narration:** “They did not know their world would one day be called Earth.”

### Shot 7 — Silence

**Time:** 01:15–01:23
**Visual:** The rain stops. Animals in the forest raise their heads simultaneously. The hominids notice the absence of sound and look toward the clearing.
**Camera:** Static cuts: animal eye, still leaves, listening face.
**Audio:** All natural sound drops away.
**Narration:** “On the final night before the Expansion, the animals fell silent.”

### Shot 8 — The false star

**Time:** 01:23–01:30
**Visual:** Beyond black tree branches, a bright silver point hangs just above the horizon. The stars rotate subtly with elapsed time; this light remains fixed. On the final second, it becomes marginally brighter.
**Camera:** Locked wide shot.
**Audio:** One deep impact at 01:29, then cut to silence.
**Narration:** “Above the horizon, one star refused to move.”

## Transition into Section 002

Section 001 ends on the stationary light. Section 002 begins with the same light seen from space, revealing Earth's moon in the foreground. Space bends behind the moon and the first Aethari vessel emerges from the Fold.
